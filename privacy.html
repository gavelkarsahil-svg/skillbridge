Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This page is a placeholder written for a college coursework project. SkillBridge is a fictional program — no real data collection, tracking, or third-party sharing takes place.

What this demo site stores

The live version of this site saves cart items, applications, and orders to your own browser's local storage only. Nothing is transmitted to a server or seen by anyone else. Clearing your browser data, or the "Clear all" buttons on this site, removes it.

Login / Sign Up

The Login and Sign Up forms on this site are a UI demonstration only. Any email and password combination is accepted, and no real account or password is created or verified anywhere.

Checkout

Card details entered at checkout are never transmitted anywhere — they exist only in the browser during the demo and are not stored, validated, or charged.

What a real version of this policy would cover

A production privacy policy would typically explain what personal information is collected (name, email, phone), why it's collected (to process bootcamp applications and orders), how long it's retained, whether it's shared with partner organizations, and how a user can request their data be deleted.

Contact

Questions about this coursework project can be directed to hello@skillbridge.example.
